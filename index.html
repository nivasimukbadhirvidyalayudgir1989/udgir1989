About Our School

Niwasi Mook Badhir Vidyalaya Udgir was established in 1989. The school is located in Jadkot road Udgir block of Latur district, Maharashtra.

The school provides education from Class 1 to Class 7. It is a co-educational school and has a pre-primary section. Marathi is the medium of instruction. School for the Hearing and Speech Impaired" or "Special School for Deaf and Mute Children"

The school is located in an urban area and is managed by a Private Aided Management.
